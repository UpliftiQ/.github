## What We Are Building

Most learning platforms add captions and call it accessibility.
We started with Indian Sign Language.

UpliftiQ is a complete learning ecosystem designed from the ground up for Deaf and hard of hearing learners. ISL is the foundation, not an afterthought.

---

## The Problem

18 million Deaf and hard of hearing people live in India.
Less than 1% receive education in Indian Sign Language.
Existing platforms treat accessibility as a feature toggle.
No platform has ever been built ISL first for Indian learners.

Source: World Health Organization and National Association of the Deaf India.

**Three pillars:**

🤟 **ISL First Learning**
Every core lesson ships with native Indian Sign Language video recorded by Deaf educators. Not auto generated. Not an afterthought.

🤖 **Upli AI**
An AI tutor that explains concepts visually, in Simple English, with ISL video links. Built for how visual learners actually think.

👥 **Community**
Study groups, peer mentorship, ISL video feed, and forums where ISL is the primary language. Not a translation of English threads.

## Our Commitment to the Deaf Community

We hold ourselves to three public commitments.

**1. Deaf Led Content Review**
Every course is reviewed and approved by a Deaf educator before launch. No exceptions. We publish review credits on every course.

**2. Open Advisory Board**
We are forming a public advisory board of Deaf educators, ISL linguists, and community leaders. Members and meeting notes will be published on our About page.

**3. Public Accessibility Reports**
Twice a year we publish a public report covering accessibility metrics, ISL content coverage, Deaf community involvement, and course completion rates. The first report will be published when our early access cohort completes its first quarter.

## Why We Build This

India has 18 million Deaf citizens and almost none of them have access to learning content in their own language. Existing platforms treat Indian Sign Language as an afterthought. A captioning toggle. A translation overlay.

We are building the platform we wish existed. ISL first. Deaf led. Built around how visual learners actually think.

Starting with India. Scaling to the world.
